# Current WOLCO media

This release replaces the earlier four generated scene families and generated film with WOLCO-specific architectural concepts and genuine supplied WOLCO project footage. The original Sanctuary layout, typography, logo, responsive picture loading and interaction IDs remain preserved.

Nine generated studies illustrate arrival, living, kitchen, material junction, bedroom, alfresco and three selection directions. They are fictional inspiration, not approved WOLCO designs, completed projects, actual studio interiors or confirmed inclusions. Each room control now shows its corresponding room. White is Always Right, Honed Travertine & Cashmere, and Espresso, Walnut & Garnet each have a dedicated matching selection image and descriptive finish records. All nine image families have AVIF and WebP at 640, 960 and 1600 pixels wide.

The approximately 21.6-second, silent 1280×720/24fps film is edited from the supplied Cookes Road project film: Arrival, Entry, Volume, Gather, Craft, Restore and Home. Subtle contrast/saturation and dissolves support web presentation; architectural features and the original WOLCO corner mark remain.
